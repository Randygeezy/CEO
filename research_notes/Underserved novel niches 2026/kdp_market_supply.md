Method note: research date 2026-11-16. Page fetches to authormedia.com and k-lytics.com failed (hosts are blocked in this session's network settings), so every figure below comes from search-engine extracts of the cited pages, not from reading the full page. ## 1. Which fiction subgenres do analysts and author communities call underserved or emerging in 2025-2026?

### Takeaway
The best data (K-lytics, summer 2026) points to growth in fantasy subgenres: LitRPG, cozy fantasy and dark fantasy. It also shows a broad science-fiction rebound (sci-fi horror and sci-fi romance score high) and techno-thrillers up 49% in mystery/thriller. "Underserved" claims for clean romance, paranormal romance and dark romance come mostly from blogs and vendors, not data. Paranormal romance is also one of the most AI-flooded categories, which casts doubt on those claims.

### Cited Findings
- K-lytics summer 2026 SF&F report, as summarized by Author Media: fantasy outsells science fiction by "nearly 4 to 1," "powered by Romantasy and, more recently, by LitRPG." It covered more than 8,000 Amazon sales categories. — [Author Media: Cozy Fantasy and LitRPG SURGE as Hottest Niches in 2026](https://www.authormedia.com/zeitgeist-cozy-fantasy-and-litrpg-surge-as-hottest-niches-in-2026/)
- The same summary says fantasy "just posted the strongest average sales rank K-lytics has ever recorded" and science fiction is "18 months into a genuine, broad-based rebound." — [Author Media (via search extract)](https://www.authormedia.com/zeitgeist-cozy-fantasy-and-litrpg-surge-as-hottest-niches-in-2026/)
- K-lytics keyword scores (summer 2026): "scifi horror" 51.35, "scifi romance" 50.33, "fantasy litrpg" 49.45, "dark fantasy" 48.15, "cozy fantasy" 45.91. "Cozy fantasy" (45.9) now outranks "epic fantasy" (41.4). — [Author Media](https://www.authormedia.com/zeitgeist-cozy-fantasy-and-litrpg-surge-as-hottest-niches-in-2026/)
- Author Media notes that K-lytics "does not say where the surge comes from" but that its timing lines up with AI-assisted publishing. This is speculation. — [Author Media](https://www.authormedia.com/zeitgeist-cozy-fantasy-and-litrpg-surge-as-hottest-niches-in-2026/)
- The K-lytics summer 2026 SF&F edition maps 183 SF&F sub-categories. Different K-lytics materials give different counts (170+, 176, 178, 183). — [K-lytics SF&F report page](https://k-lytics.com/scifi-fantasy/); [Vappingo K-lytics review](https://www.vappingo.com/word-blog/k-lytics-review/)
- K-lytics mystery/thriller/suspense report (2026): techno-thrillers were up 49% over 12 months, linked by the host to interest in AI. Political thrillers and police procedurals are rising, and assassination thrillers are falling. — [Author Media: Mystery Thriller Suspense K-Lytics Report](https://www.authormedia.com/zeitgeist-mystery-thriller-suspense-k-lytics-report/); [YouTube: "Techno Thrillers Are Up 49%"](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=B1XktxS_EtA)
- The K-lytics MTS report breaks down more than 150 sub-markets and compares KU vs. non-KU performance (sales page description). — [K-lytics MTS report](https://k-lytics.com/product/k-lytics-report-mystery-thriller-suspense/)
- The Chakrabarty et al. 2026 preprint finds that Fantasy/Supernatural/Horror was the genre cluster AI reached "latest and least," and human-authored books there gained (one post cites +35%). — [arXiv 2607.20349v3](https://arxiv.org/html/2607.20349v3); [Chakrabarty on X](https://x.com/TuhinChakr/status/2082124145312022837)
- Clean romance is described as a "loyal, underserved segment," with church book clubs buying in groups. Military and historical sub-niches are called "smaller audiences, but loyal; lower competition." These are vendor/blog claims. — [KDPBuilder fiction niches 2026](https://kdpbuilder.com/niches/fiction)
- A romance blog calls paranormal and dark romance "deep, underserved wells." This is a publisher's opinion with no data. — [PlotProse romance subgenres 2026](https://plotprose.com/romance-subgenres-2026/)
- Written Word Media 2025 Indie Author Survey (~1,346 respondents): paranormal romance authors are ~4% of respondents but ~11% of those earning over $10k/month (~2.5x expected), and ~3% of those under $100. Romance is 21% of respondents and fantasy 14%. About 44% earn ≤$100/month and ~13% earn >$5,000/month. The sample skews toward prolific, marketing-active authors. These figures come from secondary write-ups; the original results page was not reachable. — [Written Word Media 2025 survey results](https://www.writtenwordmedia.com/2025-indie-author-survey-results/)
- Cozy LitRPG is a visible micro-niche (dedicated "Best New Cozy LitRPG" lists, Aethon low-stakes cozy titles). — [Fantasyranked July 2026](https://fantasyranked.com/blog/2026-07-24-new-litrpg-progression-fantasy-releases-worth-your-time-july-2026/); [Aethon Sept 2026 launches](https://aethonbooks.com/2026/08/06/brand-new-litrpg-and-progression-fantasy-series-launching-september-2026/)

### Inferences
- On the data, the strongest "demand ahead of supply" signals are sci-fi horror and sci-fi romance (the highest K-lytics keyword scores, in a genre that is rebounding), cozy fantasy, and techno/political thrillers. Fantasy/horror is also the cluster least diluted by AI.
- The paranormal-romance evidence conflicts. It earns well (WWM) and is called underserved (blogs), but it is reportedly 62% AI junk among new releases (section 2). High income per author can coexist with a flooded new-release shelf, so "underserved" is doubtful there.
- LitRPG demand is real (Dungeon Crawler Carl, 4:1 fantasy ratio), but supply is growing fast too (section 2), so it reads as "hot" rather than clearly "underserved."

### Gaps
- No K-lytics primary report text was accessible. The keyword-score methodology (what 45.9 vs 51.35 means) is not explained in the extracts.
- No sales-rank or title-count figures for cozy fantasy, LitRPG, sci-fi horror or sci-fi romance specifically.
- No findings for military SF or space opera growth in 2026.
- Nothing found from Bryan Cohen, Alessandra Torre, Dave Chesson/Publisher Rocket, 20BooksTo50K successors or Kboards naming underserved subgenres in 2025-2026.

## 2. Which genres or categories are saturated or flooded, including by AI-generated books?

### Takeaway
Romance is still the largest genre but appears to be maturing. Paranormal romance has the most specific AI-flood figure (62% of September new releases likely AI junk, per K-lytics). Academic studies show AI text spreading across all self-published genre fiction: about 1 in 5 books carries substantial AI text, and AI books took 31% of new top-25 entries. Fantasy/supernatural/horror is the exception.

### Cited Findings
- Author Media citing K-lytics: romance makes up roughly 70% of Kindle ebook sales. In a later piece, romance's share of the top 100 US Kindle books fell "from close to 60 percent last year to 48 percent this year" (romantasy included), though it is still the largest genre. Contemporary romance leads historical. — [Author Media: Romance Dominates Kindle But Shows Signs of Maturing](https://www.authormedia.com/zeitgeist-romance-dominates-kindle-but-shows-signs-of-maturing/). Note: a follow-up search could not independently verify the 60%→48% figure.
- Eight of the top 100 books were Dungeon Crawler Carl titles at one point in 2026 (Author Media, via search extract), which shows LitRPG crowding into the top 100. — [Author Media](https://www.authormedia.com/zeitgeist-romance-dominates-kindle-but-shows-signs-of-maturing/)
- K-lytics data, as cited by Author Media: "62% of new paranormal romance titles released in September were likely junk AI books." The report profiles 7,050 titles within a ~32,000-title market. Also: "what used to be two books a month can now become thirty books a month." This is a single-source estimate; K-lytics' own page for the October 2025 paranormal report does not show the stat. — [Author Media: Reports Say 62% of New Paranormal Romance Titles Are AI Junk Books](https://www.authormedia.com/reports-say-62-of-new-paranormal-romance-titles-are-ai-junk-books/); [K-lytics paranormal romance report](https://k-lytics.com/product/paranormal-romance-books/)
- K-lytics' AI classification (billionaire romance report) combines signals into a quantitative score and admits false positives and missed AI books. — [AIWEAF Substack](https://aiweaf.substack.com/p/if-the-slop-is-ranking-i-still-want)
- Chakrabarty, Liu, Ginsburg & Dhillon (arXiv 2607.20349, 2026 preprint, not peer-reviewed; Pangram detector): 14,419 self-published genre-fiction ebooks on Amazon, 2023 to March 2026, with sales data through June 2026.
  - ~20% (2,880 titles) have "substantial" AI text (>25% of prose flagged).
  - 2026 Q1 sales share: 36.6% any AI text; 17.5% substantial AI text.
  - The share of new top-25 entries with substantial AI content rose from near 0 to 31%.
  - In 7 of 8 genres, revenue per title fell even for non-AI books.
  - The non-AI hold on top-chart spots slides from ~88% in the least-AI genres to ~63% in the most-AI genres.
  - Non-AI books lost most where AI diffusion and KU availability were high.
  - Sources: [arXiv HTML](https://arxiv.org/html/2607.20349); [arXiv v3](https://arxiv.org/html/2607.20349v3); [SSRN](https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/Delivery.cfm/7159278.pdf?abstractid=7159278&mirid=1&type=2); [Entangled Text coverage](https://www.entangledtext.com/news/study-finds-ai-generated-books-are-crowding-amazon-and-cutting-author-pay)
- An a16z summary says books with any detectable AI text were ~40% of observed self-pub Amazon sales. This is secondary and does not match the paper's 36.6% figure. — [Gate.com reporting a16z](https://www.gate.com/news/detail/a16z-self-published-ai-books-on-amazon-account-for-40-of-sales-diluting-23504033)
- Reimers & Waldfogel, NBER w34777: monthly Amazon book releases roughly tripled from 2022 to late 2025 (some categories ~10x), reaching ~300,000/month by late 2025. Detected AI use was ~0 through 2022, ~30% in 2023, ~45% in 2024 and >60% in 2025. AI books get fewer ratings, lower stars and worse ranks. This covers all books, not fiction only. — [NBER digest](https://www.nber.org/digest/202605/how-large-language-models-are-reshaping-book-market); [NBER paper](https://www.nber.org/papers/w34777); [Conversable Economist](https://conversableeconomist.com/2026/05/19/how-is-ai-affecting-the-quantity-and-quality-of-new-books/)
- Fortune (Sept 14, 2026): "thousands of AI slop books flood online marketplaces, human authors are getting squeezed." — [Fortune](https://fortune.com/2026/09/14/ai-slop-books-amazon-marketplace-publishing-human-authors/)
- KDP policy: a cap of 3 new titles per account per day (introduced 2023, older data), with AI disclosure to Amazon required but not shown to buyers. — [Authors Guild](https://authorsguild.org/news/amazon-adds-to-kdp-generative-ai-policy-caps-daily-self-publishing-uploads/); [Gizmodo](https://gizmodo.com/amazon-restricts-author-self-publishing-3-books-a-day-1850864471)
- LitRPG supply is growing fast: Aethon runs monthly launch slates through 2026. Community-sourced LitRPGTools data says translated Russian LitRPG is >30% of new releases tracked in the genre monthly (methodology unverified). — [Aethon](https://aethonbooks.com/2026/06/09/brand-new-litrpg-and-progression-fantasy-series-launching-july-2026/); [Fantasyranked](https://fantasyranked.com/blog/2026-07-24-new-litrpg-progression-fantasy-releases-worth-your-time-july-2026/)
- Mafia/arranged-marriage romance is described as "dominant" in 2026 lists. The source is a mafia-romance author's own blog, which shows prevalence rather than measuring saturation. — [Gabrielle Sands](https://www.gabriellesands.com/best-mafia-romance-2026/)
- A 2026 stats roundup says romantic comedy and new adult are "softening" as readers move to more intense stories (US romance print units 44M in 2025). — [SelfPublishing.com Fiction by the Numbers 2026](https://selfpublishing.com/fiction-by-the-numbers-2026/)

### Inferences
- The saturation mechanism has changed. AI floods do not mainly take top slots from human books. They dilute discoverability and revenue per title, and the damage is worst in high-KU, formulaic romance subgenres (paranormal, billionaire).
- Fantasy/horror being the least AI-diluted cluster supports the K-lytics fantasy-strength signal. It is the most defensible "human-advantage" space as of mid-2026.

### Gaps
- No per-genre AI-share table could be extracted from the arXiv paper (the eight clusters are named only partially: "General Romance," "Speculative/Adventure," "Fantasy/Supernatural/Horror").
- No hard data on dark/mafia romance saturation (title counts, review distributions).

## 3. What metrics do authors use to judge demand vs. supply, and what do recent analyses show?

### Takeaway
The usual method is to pull the top 10-20 titles for a category or keyword and check their BSRs and review counts. Published thresholds conflict and come mostly from non-fiction/low-content vendors. The Kindle Unlimited → Kindle+ change on Oct 22, 2026 has made KU page-read economics harder to judge.

### Cited Findings
- Check the BSR of the #20 book in a category. If it is under 50,000, the category has "consistent, healthy daily sales." — [Low Content Profits (2026)](https://lowcontentprofits.com/amazon-kdp-niche-research-for-beginners/)
- Other published thresholds: 3-5 of the top 10 should have BSR <100,000. A stricter "3-3-3 check" wants 3 sub-niche titles with BSR <150,000 and all under 30 reviews. — [KDP Niche Hunter](https://www.kdpnichehunter.com/blog/how-to-find-profitable-kdp-niches); [The Publishing Times](https://thepublishingtimes.com/blog/how-to-validate-your-book-idea-before-you-write-it-using-sales-intelligence-data-1788197948177)
- Top-ranking books averaging under 50 reviews signals low competition. If the top 5 have 1,000+ reviews, "the barrier to entry is high." — [Inkfluence AI](https://www.inkfluenceai.com/learn/high-demand-low-competition-ebook-niches)
- Example of a saturation score: "dog training book" scored 30 (saturated), with median BSR 15,900 but median 2,695 reviews in the top 10. — [Apify KDP Niche Analyzer](https://apify.com/tagadanar/kdp-niche-analyzer)
- K-lytics measures average sales rank per category, KU vs. non-KU share, keyword scores, and estimated sales. Vappingo cautions that these are estimates, since Amazon does not publish competitors' sales; reports cost ~$37-47 each in 2026. — [Vappingo K-lytics review](https://www.vappingo.com/word-blog/k-lytics-review/)
- Kindle Unlimited was renamed Kindle+ on Oct 22, 2026. Eligible audiobooks were added, paid from the same KDP Select Global Fund. KENP reports now split pages read vs. pages listened. — [Written Word Media: Kindle+](https://www.writtenwordmedia.com/kindle-plus-for-authors/); [Author Media](https://www.authormedia.com/kindle-unlimited-is-dead-long-live-kindle/)
- Authors reported KENP estimator figures 40-50% lower overnight, with one claiming >70% lost in October. These are anecdotal forum reports. — [Author Media: Authors Lost 40-70% of KENP Overnight](https://www.authormedia.com/authors-lost-40-70-of-kenp-overnight/)
- KENP rate is reported at roughly $0.0041-0.0054 per page in 2025-2026 (secondary source). — [SelfPubGo KENP calculator](https://selfpubgo.com/tools/kenp-calculator)
- Literary fiction is weaker in KU because readers buy rather than borrow. — [Ecommerce Paradise / KU strategy blogs](https://getebook.ai/blog/kindle-unlimited-strategy)

### Inferences
- BSR and review thresholds were designed for non-fiction. In fiction, series read-through and KU borrows inflate BSR for a given income, so a sub-50k #20 BSR is a weak fiction filter. In AI-flooded categories, low review counts in the top 100 may reflect AI churn rather than open demand.
- After Kindle+, a category's KU share is a less reliable demand proxy, because audiobook listening now draws on the same pot. Authors without audio may see lower per-page payouts.

### Gaps
- No 2025-2026 Kindlepreneur/Publisher Rocket fiction-specific competition-score guidance was found.
- No official Nov 2026 Global Fund/KENP figure was found.

## 4. Which 2025-2026 BookTok and reader-platform trends has indie supply not yet filled?

### Takeaway
Trend forecasts (mostly written late 2025) name dark romantasy, romance-horror/"femgore," sci-fi with romance, Viking/pirate/sports/dystopian romantasy, cozy fantasy and "weird girl" books. Of these, only sci-fi romance and sci-fi horror have hard K-lytics support for strong demand. No source measures indie supply for these trends.

### Cited Findings
- The Bookseller's BookTok 2026 genre predictions: romantasy holds, and some creators think epic fantasy/SF could overtake it. Cozy fantasy and "weird girl" books are named as possible breakouts. — [The Bookseller](https://www.thebookseller.com/books/books-on-booktok-2026-genre-predictions)
- Atmosphere Press: romantasy "isn't dying, but the heat is moving" toward darker, morally complex villain romance, dark romantasy, and psychological suspense hybrids. — [Atmosphere Press](https://atmospherepress.com/romantasy-trends-2026/)
- Agent Alyssa Matesic lists dystopian romantasy and sports romantasy, with Viking-inspired and pirate romantasy as emerging settings. She also notes that self-published Kindle hits are being acquired by traditional publishers. — [Alyssa Matesic: Book Genre Trends 2026](https://www.alyssamatesic.com/free-writing-resources/book-genre-trends)
- Romance-horror, female-rage horror ("femgore"), literary horror and weird fiction are named as rising, alongside SF with a strong romance subplot. — [Chapter.pub BookTok trends 2026](https://blog.chapter.pub/booktok-trends-2026/); [BookBrowse 2026 predictions](https://www.bookbrowse.com/blogs/editor/index.cfm/2025/12/15/Reading-and-Publishing-Predictions-Book-Trends-to-Watch-for-in-2026)
- *Heated Rivalry* (hockey M/M romance) was #2 in the January 2026 Kindle paid romance top 100, and was among Goodreads' most-read romances. — [Romancing the Data, Jan 2026](https://blog.romancingthedata.com/p/amazon-kindle-store-romance-best-sellers-january-2026); [Goodreads](https://www.goodreads.com/blog/show/3130-readers-most-read-books-of-the-reading-challenge-so-far)

### Inferences
- Where trend forecasts overlap with K-lytics keyword strength, the case is strongest for sci-fi romance and sci-fi horror (scores 50+) and for horror-adjacent fantasy (the least AI-diluted cluster). Sports/hockey M/M romance has a chart signal (*Heated Rivalry*) but no supply data.

### Gaps
- No source quantifies indie title counts against demand for any BookTok trend, so "not yet filled" cannot be confirmed with numbers.
- Most trend pieces date from Dec 2025 and are forecasts. No late-2026 retrospective was found.

## 5. What practical advice exists for validating a niche before writing?

### Takeaway
The standard "write to market" workflow is to pick a sub-category, study the top 20-100 for BSR, review counts, covers, tropes and series status, mine 3-star reviews for unmet wants, and check that several recent, low-review books sell well. Paid tools (K-lytics, Publisher Rocket, Kindletrends) give category-level trend data.

### Cited Findings
- Read all reviews of the competing top books, especially 3-star ones: "those tell you what's missing." — [The Publishing Times (2026)](https://thepublishingtimes.com/blog/how-to-validate-your-book-idea-before-you-write-it-using-sales-intelligence-data-1788197948177)
- Look for recent, low-review titles with good BSRs (the 3-3-3 check) as evidence that newcomers can break in. — [KDP Niche Hunter](https://www.kdpnichehunter.com/blog/how-to-find-profitable-kdp-niches)
- Kindletrends' weekly romance newsletter gives top-100 composition data (used in the Jan 2026 analysis). — [Romancing the Data](https://blog.romancingthedata.com/p/amazon-kindle-store-romance-best-sellers-january-2026)
- K-lytics' YA analysis (older, pre-2025): top sellers stay on top for a long time, and series do best. — [Jane Friedman: Amazon trend report teen/YA](https://janefriedman.com/amazon-trend-report-teen-and-young-adult/)
- Vendor sources say to treat "underserved" as a hypothesis to test against bestseller lists and reviews. — [KDPBuilder](https://kdpbuilder.com/niches/fiction)

### Inferences
- Since mid-2025, validation also needs an AI-contamination check. A category with many low-review new releases may be AI churn (per the 62% paranormal finding), not open demand. Look at the age and review velocity of the human-written books in the top 20.

### Gaps
- No 2025-2026 primary write-to-market guidance (Chris Fox, 20BooksTo50K successors, Kboards) was retrievable.
- Reddit threads from r/selfpublish and r/selfpublishing were not surfaced by search.
