# Money X-Ray Action Kit: cold read as a print-and-handwrite buyer

Read all 17 pages as images (70 dpi, p10 at 200 dpi). Measured the form-field boxes with pypdf.

## Kit-wide
- **Boxes are short for handwriting.** Most are 13-15 pt tall (about 5 mm); thermometer rows are 11 pt (about 4 mm). Comfortable handwriting needs 7-8 mm. Row spacing is about 22 pt, so you can write over the box edges, but the boxes look cramped. Some are narrow too: p5 Monthly is 50 pt and p8 APR is 55 pt, tight for "$1,234.56".
- **Labels are tiny, widely spaced grey capitals** (about 6 pt) on a cream background. They will print faint on a home inkjet.
- **The kit assumes the AI fills it.** The cover, p2, p10, p13 and p14 all say "the AI writes/picks/sets". By hand, nothing tells you how to work the numbers out yourself.
- **Page count is wrong.** The cover says "Fifteen pages"; the PDF has 17.
- **Debt slots don't match.** p8 has 8 debts, p9 has 5 columns and p10 has 4 thermometers.
- **Printing cost.** The cover (p1) is solid black. p16-17 are code and field lists for the AI. Skip all three when printing.

## Top fixes
1. Make boxes at least 20 pt tall, or drop the boxes and use ruled lines.
2. Add a one-line "by hand" rule to p10, p13 and p14 (for example, 90% paid = start balance x 0.9).
3. Explain Method, Cushion, FLAG and "$0.00 check".
4. Fix "Fifteen pages".
5. Give the debt name boxes on p9 and p10 headers or placeholders.
6. Say "print pages 2-15".
