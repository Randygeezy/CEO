# Guide vs reality: Money X-Ray, free plan, iPhone, no code tool

"Guide pN" means a page of Money_XRay_Complete_Guide.pdf; "Kit pN" means a page of Money_XRay_Action_Kit.pdf. Full run: transcript.md (34 messages).

## Outcome in one paragraph
The guide's order worked: paste prompt, upload, done, go, go, go, answer, menu. Every account balanced to $0.00 with hand arithmetic, and every headline number matched the guide's own sample report for this household to the cent: spending $5,407.16/mo, left over $1,407.84, September $5,926.51, fees and interest $522.59, Joint up $4,740, cuts $29.98/mo ($270 short), lump sum $2,587.08, net worth $162,554.08, liquid $154.08 (guide p27, p33-35). All planted problems were caught: 3 repeated Visa rows, the DoorDash double charge, the hidden Discover card and savings account, the Netflix price rise, overlapping services, SQ *JMK LLC, the new checking fee, Costco relabelled as groceries, and the Zelle payback. The Action Kit was only partly usable. On a free plan the AI gives values for 5 of its 13 working pages. The buyer then types about 60 values into small PDF boxes by hand, and the debt-free date and interest boxes stay empty.

## Things that matched exactly
- Table of contents page numbers (guide p2) all point to the right pages.
- Guide p16 sample questions are almost word for word what the AI asked (J.M. $45, SQ *JMK LLC, DISCOVER $175, cash). There were 8 questions, within the stated limit.
- Guide p33 trust table: 3 repeated Visa rows, a Mastercard double charge, and Discover and savings not uploaded. All matched.
- Guide p34-35: subscriptions list, Netflix $15.49 to $17.99, Planet Fitness $348.88 a year, fees list, avalanche order Discover, Visa, car, family. All matched.
- Guide p25: lump sum $2,587.08 ($5,699.99 - $2,000 - $1,112.91). Matched.
- Guide p27: $10,980 cash, $60,500 retirement, $364,500 home and cars, -$273,426 owed, $162,554 net worth, $154 liquid. Matched.
- Guide p13 "You shouldn't have to push it": true. The AI never needed "are you done?".

## Steps where a phone beginner would get stuck (in order)
1. Guide p8-9: getting CSVs on an iPhone. The app gives PDFs; CSV needs the bank website.
2. Guide p9: card CSVs have no balances, and the guide doesn't say what to do if PDFs aren't an option. A real buyer has no summary file.
3. Guide p12: copying the whole 25 KB prompt on iPhone. No how-to. Attaching it instead uses a scarce free upload.
4. Guide p13 / p18: upload limits. 7 to 8 uploads in one day. The fallback "open the CSV, copy the rows" doesn't work well on iPhone, because Files shows CSVs as a preview table, not copyable text.
5. Guide p17: typing one long reply on a phone covering 5 options with all the details.
6. Guide p17: saving 8 CSV code blocks as .csv files on iPhone. No built-in way.
7. Guide p26: "use any debt payoff calculator" with a lump sum and rollover. No named tool or steps.
8. Guide p17 / Kit p2: uploading a 0.8 MB PDF into an already very long free chat. Likely to hit the chat or upload limit.
9. Kit p2-3: typing about 60 values into PDF form boxes while switching between apps. The other 8 pages are left blank.
