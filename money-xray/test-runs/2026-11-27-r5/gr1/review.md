# Cold read: Money_XRay_Complete_Guide.pdf (45 pages, read on a phone)

Reader: bought the guide, reading on a phone, has never downloaded a bank statement and barely used an AI chat app.

## Verdict

It's friendly and well ordered, and the "fastest path" on p4 is a good idea. I'd still get stuck at the very first action, though. Nobody tells me how to open the .txt prompt on a phone, copy 25 KB of it and paste it into ChatGPT. The guide also points to a file I don't have (Money_XRay_Prompt.pdf) and a third file it never lists (the Action Kit PDF). The bigger issue is the format: every page is A4 and the text is a desk size. At phone width, body text comes out around 6.5pt, and each page needs a pinch-zoom and a side-to-side pan. All the screenshots are too small to read. A newcomer can finish it but will be slow and nervous doing it. I give it about 6.5/10 for this reader.

## Top 5 problems

1. **Copying the prompt on a phone is never explained (p7, p12, p40).** "Paste the whole prompt from Money_XRay_Prompt.txt" assumes I know where that file landed (email? Downloads? Files app?), how to open a .txt on iPhone or Android, how to select all of it and how to paste into the chat. This is step 1 of the whole product, and it's the place I'd most likely give up.
2. **The files don't match.** p7 says "The PDF and text file that came with this guide". p12 says I can attach "Money_XRay_Prompt.pdf, not this guide", but my download has only the guide and the .txt. p17 and p31 talk about an "Action Kit" fillable PDF that p7's "What you need" list never mentions. Where do I get it? Is it missing?
3. **It's too small to read on a phone everywhere (all pages).** Measured sizes on an A4 page, then scaled to about 0.65 for a phone: body text 10pt comes out around 6.5pt, table text 9pt around 5.9pt, grey captions and notes 7.8 to 8.6pt around 5pt, and spaced-out capital table headers 6.8pt around 4.4pt. The appendix monospace (7.6pt, p40 to 45) is about 5pt, and the footers about 4pt. Every page has to be zoomed and panned.
4. **The screenshots can't be read and don't help (p8, 9, 12, 13, 14, 16, 19, 21, 25, 28).** The phone images are shrunk to about a third of the page, so the menu labels in them are tiny and most of their text is blurred or blacked out. The p12 "A fresh, empty chat" image is almost entirely black. Several captions say "earlier version" (p12, 16, 19, 25), which makes me wonder whether I'm looking at the right thing.
5. **Words are used before they're explained.** CSV appears on p7 and p8 but isn't explained until p9. "Save block" appears on p4 and isn't explained until p19. "Code tool / run the math in code" appears on p7 and is only half-explained on p18. "Liquid net worth" appears on p6 and is explained on p27. "Idle cash, cushion" appear on p17 and are explained on p24 to p26. Some words are never explained at all (list below). The glossary on p38 is at the very back, and nothing early on tells me it's there.

## Too small to read on a phone (measured)

The page is A4 (596pt wide). On a typical phone screen of about 390pt, the scale is about 0.65.

| Element | PDF size | On phone |
|---|---|---|
| Body text | 10pt | ~6.5pt |
| Table text | 9pt | ~5.9pt |
| Grey notes / captions | 7.8 to 8.6pt | ~5 to 5.6pt |
| Table headers (spaced caps) | 6.8pt | ~4.4pt |
| Footers | 6.5pt | ~4.2pt |
| Appendix prompt | 7.6pt mono | ~4.9pt |
| Text inside screenshots | n/a | unreadable |

Comfortable phone reading is about 11pt and up at screen size, so every page needs zooming.

## What worked

- p4 "The fastest path" and "You're not bad with money" set a calm tone.
- p13 "You shouldn't have to push it" and p16 "Reply ok to keep all its guesses" take the pressure off.
- p22 and p23 copy-paste scripts for cancelling and asking for a fee back are practical.
- p33 to p35 sample report makes the payoff concrete.
- p39 checklist is the best single page in the guide.

## Suggested fixes (short)

1. Add a one-page "Copy the prompt on your phone" walkthrough (iPhone and Android) with large screenshots, right after p4.
2. Fix the file list: say exactly which files come in the download (guide, .txt, Action Kit?) and remove or supply Money_XRay_Prompt.pdf.
3. Make a phone edition (a narrow page about 5.5in wide, or reflowable) with body text at 14pt or more. Or at least enlarge the screenshots to full width and crop them to the relevant part.
4. Explain CSV, save block, code tool and card issuer at first use, and put a "magic words" box (done, go, ok, continue, monthly checkup, fill my kit) on p12.
5. Move the checklist and the sample report forward. Cut the four divider pages, and shrink or move the appendix prompt (it's in the .txt anyway).
