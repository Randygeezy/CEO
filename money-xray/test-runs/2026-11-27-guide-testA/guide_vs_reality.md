Test run of the v3 (statements-first) flow with a code tool. Page numbers are the printed page numbers of `Money_XRay_Complete_Guide.pdf` (they match the PDF page index). The customer read chapters 1 to 10 (pages 4 to 21) before starting. Pages from chapters 11 to 21 and the appendix were checked afterwards, only to compare results; they are marked "(outside ch. 1-10)".

## Places where the guide matched reality

- Report section order and the 9 section names (p.16): matched exactly.
- $0.00 check on every account (p.6): all five accounts balanced to $0.00 before any finding. The Visa balances only after removing 3 repeated rows (off by $51.44 with them), as p.6 describes.
- 8 or fewer questions, each with a guess; "ok" works (p.18): matched (8 items).
- Cash handled once, weekly turned into monthly, ATM cash not double counted, kept as Customer-reported (p.19): matched.
- Fees and interest $522.59 (p.25), net worth $162,554 and liquid $154 (p.29), lump sum $2,587.08 (p.27): all matched to the cent ($162,554.08, $154.08).
- PDF report made by the assistant on a code-capable plan (p.8): matched.

## Prompt issues found along the way (not guide wording, but they affect what the customer sees)

- Step 1 says "Keep it under 150 words" but also requires two long passages verbatim. With those passages the shortest faithful message was 175 words.
- Step 7 "After the report" plus Step 4 "Right after the report, ask..." leaves it unclear whether the save block or the questions come first; this run put the save block first.
- The Visa APR estimate formula ("monthly interest / balance x 12") doesn't say which balance; using the closing balance gives 22.98%, while the balance on the interest date gives about 23.75%.
- The Discover "$175 vs $65 minimum" question is moot under both methods here, because the one-time payment clears Discover in month 0 or 1.
