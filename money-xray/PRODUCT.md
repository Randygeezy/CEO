# Money X-Ray

## What it is

A one-time-purchase digital product. Customers paste a master prompt into the AI they already use (ChatGPT, Claude or Gemini, free plans included), upload their own bank and credit card statements, and get a verified report of where their money goes, a plan for their subscriptions and fees, and a debt payoff plan. An optional Notion dashboard keeps the results month to month. There's no app, no bank linking and no monthly fee.

Working tagline: "Find out where your money really goes."

## Who it's for

- People who earn decent money and still feel broke at the end of the month. The test household is the average US family: $7,290 a month take-home, a mortgage, a car loan, three credit cards and a Visa balance of about $6,500 at 22.99% APR.
- Viewers of the founder's financial storytelling channel on YouTube, Shorts and Instagram Reels. They arrive from a video, already recognizing their own problem, and buy through Stan Store.
- Not finance people. They don't know what APR, reconciliation or a CSV is until the product explains it. Many are on a phone.

## Brand and tone

- A calm investigator who shows the evidence. Every number traces back to a real transaction.
- Honest about limits. If the cuts fall short of the goal, it says by how much.
- No shame and no lectures about spending.
- Plain words and short sentences. Money terms are explained the first time.
- The founder's voice comes through the storytelling channel: direct, relatable, "you work hard for your money, do you know where it goes?"

## Anti-references

- Budgeting apps that ask you to link your bank and pay monthly.
- Get-rich-quick finance influencers: no Lamborghinis, no "secret hacks", no guaranteed savings.
- Bank websites: cold, legal-heavy, full of fine print.
- Generic AI-prompt packs sold by the hundred on Etsy and Gumroad.

## Principles for anything designed

1. Show the real result first: a sample report with fictional, clearly labelled data, the "found $X a year" number, and the debt-free date.
2. Make trust visible: balance checks to $0.00, "no bank linking", "your statements never pass through us", and a not-financial-advice note that is easy to find.
3. One clear action per screen. The storefront's job is the purchase. The product's job is "Copy, upload, follow the steps."
4. Phone first. Most buyers come from Shorts and Reels.
5. Never claim 100% accuracy or guaranteed savings. All demo data is fictional and labelled.
