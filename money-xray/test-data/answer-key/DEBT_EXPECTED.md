# Debt payoff plan: expected answers

Generated by `debt_plan.py`. Fictional customer. Educational, not financial advice.

## Debts the customer reports

| Debt | Balance | APR | Minimum | Interest per month now |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Discover | $2,140.00 | 27.24% | $65.00 | $48.58 |
| Visa Rewards | $6,477.85 | 22.99% | $195.00 | $124.10 |
| Toyota car loan | $14,600.00 | 6.90% | $419.00 | $83.95 |
| Mom (family loan) | $450.00 | 0.00% | $50.00 | $0.00 |

Mastercard Cash Back and Amex Blue are paid in full each month, so they are not debt in the plan (no interest is charged while the full statement balance is paid).

Plan inputs: $300.00 extra per month, plus a one-time $2,587.08 from Joint Checking ($5,699.99 minus the $2,000 cushion the customer keeps, minus the $1,112.91 Mastercard bill Joint Checking pays next). Savings ($3,200) and the cash jar are left alone. A plan that uses $3,699.99 because the Mastercard bill was already paid is also correct if the AI says it assumed that.

## Results

| Method | Order | Debt-free in | Total interest | Payoff month per debt |
|---|---|---:|---:|---|
| Avalanche (highest APR first) | Discover > Visa Rewards > Toyota car loan > Mom (family loan) | 23 months | $2,007.48 | Discover: lump sum, Visa Rewards: month 12, Toyota car loan: month 23, Mom (family loan): month 9 |
| Snowball (smallest balance first) | Mom (family loan) > Discover > Visa Rewards > Toyota car loan | 23 months | $2,061.33 | Mom (family loan): lump sum, Discover: month 1, Visa Rewards: month 12, Toyota car loan: month 23 |
| Minimums only, no lump sum, no rollover | Visa Rewards > Discover > Toyota car loan > Mom (family loan) | 62 months | $7,503.15 | Visa Rewards: month 54, Discover: month 62, Toyota car loan: month 39, Mom (family loan): month 9 |

Avalanche saves $53.85 in interest compared with snowball, and $5,495.67 compared with paying minimums only.

## What the AI must get right

- First target under avalanche is **Discover (27.24%)**, not the Visa, even though the Visa has the larger balance.
- First target under snowball is the **$450 family loan**.
- The car loan (6.9%) is last under avalanche.
- Cards paid in full each month are not in the plan.
- It says to stop new charges on the cards being paid off (or pay new charges in full).
- It keeps the customer's $2,000 cushion and doesn't touch savings or the jar without asking.
- It presents both methods and leaves the choice to the customer. No guarantees, not financial advice.
- Numbers within a few dollars and a month of the table count as correct (rounding and timing conventions differ).
